# Compare.Live

> Search globally. Buy locally.

Compare.Live is an ultra-minimalist, real-time product search engine built to simplify online shopping. We eliminate the hassle of manually checking multiple browser tabs to find the best product deals in your market.

## What We Do

The digital marketplace is highly fragmented. Identical tech gadgets, consumer electronics, and appliances often carry significantly different prices depending on which platform you visit. 

Compare.Live acts as an intelligent discovery layer between shoppers and retailers:

* **Real-Time Search:** We instantly scan the verified inventories of premium local e-commerce stores the exact moment you type a query.
* **Instant Sorting:** We unify messy product information and list available options side-by-side, sorted from the absolute lowest base price to the highest.
* **Zero Bias, Zero Friction:** We do not carry inventory, run warehouses, or handle fulfillment. Our only goal is to provide a clean, uncluttered interface that saves you time and money.
* **Personalized Trends:** Discover curated popular items based on localized aggregate trends right from the home screen to see what is currently dominating the market.

## How to Explore

1. Select your target region using the location badge.
2. Type any model, brand, or specification into the central search input.
3. Compare live prices and click directly through to the merchant storefront to make a purchase.
